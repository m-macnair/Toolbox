
#Rsync allows for many features that, when moving large collections of files, make it highly preferable to cp


rsync -av --remove-source-files /path/to/source /path/to/destination

-a preserves the directory structure, permissions, and timestamps.
-v enables verbose output to see the progress of the transfer.
--remove-source-files removes the source files after a successful transfer.
